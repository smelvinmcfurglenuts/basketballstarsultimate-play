# Basketball Stars Ultimate

**Play: [https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/smelvinmcfurglenuts/basketballstarsultimate-play/index.svg](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/smelvinmcfurglenuts/basketballstarsultimate-play/index.svg)**

A free basketball game for your browser: play the computer, a friend on the same keyboard, or someone online.
This repository holds the playable files of version 3.2.1. Online play, bug reports and name reports go to the game's
own server, the same one every copy of the game uses.

Found a bug? Use REPORT A BUG on the pause screen or the Credits screen, or email basketballstarsultimate@gmail.com.

Basketball Stars Ultimate is an unofficial fan project by G. Needham. Basketball Stars, Basketball Stars 2026 and their
characters, artwork and courts are the property of MadPuffers. Not affiliated with or endorsed by MadPuffers, the NBA,
its teams or its players.
